A still-parked session requires an explicitly authorized reload to load corrected code, inspection of its current claim/persistence/provider/hold state, and supported recovery in that owning session (for list-policy work, `/list resume`). Reconcile existing completed evidence/settlement before requesting another verdict. Do not resume or duplicate an audit already running, and never edit a journal to force recovery.
