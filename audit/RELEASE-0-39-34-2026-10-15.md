# Release 0.39.34 — 2026-10-15

## Scope since v0.39.33

Auditor fallback policy and honest settings display:

- `resolveAuditorModel` enforces forbidden-model policy at the resolved
  canonical provider/model boundary, including the implicit session last
  resort; bare aliases can no longer hide a forbidden provider-qualified
  ref, and an exhausted/forbidden chain returns an actionable
  no-eligible-model error instead of resurrecting a forbidden session
  (`extensions/loops/goal-settings-ui.ts`).
- Fallback-only auditor configuration is labeled `fallback chain (first
  eligible)` rather than `session model`; headless thinking reports
  pending until per-model dispatch instead of claiming the session cap
  (`extensions/settings-menu.ts`, `extensions/goal-commands.ts`,
  `extensions/goal-settings.ts`, `docs/SETTINGS.md` with a new Auditor
  backup selection section).
- Five regression tests: three forbidden-ladder cases, one menu-label
  case, one headless-dispatcher case
  (`tests/auditor-fallback-unification.test.ts`,
  `tests/settings-menu-complete.test.ts`,
  `tests/auditor-thinking-display.test.ts`).
- Fleet review record: `audit/PROJECT-FLEET-AND-GLLA-LESSONS-2026-10-15.md`
  (read-only; no other project reloaded or resumed).
- Post-release attr/ignore cleanup (`aa5827f1`, 13 redundant
